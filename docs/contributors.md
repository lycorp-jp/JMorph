# Contributors

We thank everyone who contributed to JMorph.

## Listing Policy

- Contributors are identified using the public name or account of their choice, such as a GitHub account, another public account, a name, or a pseudonym.
- Maintainers may decline identifiers that are misleading, impersonate others, violate applicable law, or contain abusive, discriminatory, obscene, or otherwise inappropriate content. In such cases, the contributor may choose another identifier or be acknowledged anonymously.
- A contributor's preferred identifier is published only with their explicit consent.
- Internal contributors are listed individually only after their consent has been confirmed.
- Internal contributors whose consent to be named has not been confirmed may be acknowledged anonymously without including identifying information.
- This list is not exhaustive.
- Contributors may request that their listing be updated or removed.
- Contributors are listed alphabetically by their displayed identifier; the order does not indicate the relative significance of their contributions.

## Internal Project Contributors

We gratefully acknowledge the past and present colleagues who contributed to the internal experimental project from which part of JMorph originated.

If you are one of these contributors and would be comfortable being listed by name or public account, please contact the project maintainers.
